Formal foundation

Finite Automaton & Regular Language

M = (Q, Σ, δ, q₀, F)
QSet of states
ΣInput alphabet
δTransition function
q₀Initial state
FAccepting states
Core idea

A regular expression describes a regular language. A finite automaton can recognize strings belonging to that language. REGEX-X makes that recognition process visible.

Alphabet

A finite set of symbols used to construct strings.

Character Set

A defined group of symbols allowed by a pattern.

Repetition

+, *, and {m,n} express repeated occurrences.

Concatenation

Patterns placed next to each other form a sequence.

Alternation

The | operator represents a choice between alternatives.

Regular Language

A language describable by a regular expression and recognizable by a finite automaton.
